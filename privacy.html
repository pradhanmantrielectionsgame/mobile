Privacy Policy

PradhanMantri Elections Game · last updated 4 September 2026

Short version: this game has no accounts and no login. It does not collect, store, or share any personal information about you. There are no ads and nothing is sold to anyone.

This policy covers the game at pradhanmantrielectionsgame.com and its Android app, both of which run the same code.

Analytics

The game uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly visit counter. It sets no cookies and does no cross-site or cross-app tracking. It records only aggregate, non-identifying data: a page-view count, the referring website, your browser type, and your country (derived from your IP address, which is not itself stored). This is used only to see roughly how many people play.

Data stored on your device

The game saves a few things locally in your browser or app storage so it works the way you left it:

  • Your sound and settings preferences
  • Which politicians you have unlocked
  • A replay of your most recent game

This information never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else. Clearing the site's data in your browser, or uninstalling the app, deletes all of it.

Offline cache

The game registers a service worker that stores its own files (code, images, sounds) on your device so it can run without an internet connection. This cache contains only the game itself, no data about you.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

Besides GoatCounter (described above), the game loads no third-party advertising, tracking, or social-media scripts.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change with it.
