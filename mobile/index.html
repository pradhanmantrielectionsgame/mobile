How to play

Tap a state to select it, double-tap to invest. Delhi, Goa, and the other Small UTs invest through the corner buttons the same way. Craft rally tokens into a Special Powerup or Nationwide Rally from the token tray. Commit funds to your politician's agendas from the agenda tray. Cross 272 of 543 seats to win.
